audio distributed with Titans of War. It is a release record, not legal advice.

## Historical Media And Audio

Historical paintings, prints, maps, photographs, and recordings retain the
rights status shown in:

- `src/game/mediaCatalog.js`
- `src/game/audioCatalog.js`
- `src/game/historicalAssetManifest.js`

Public-domain works are not placed under CC BY 4.0. Third-party works retain
their stated licenses and required credits.

Each shipped audio recording is tied to its audited source download by a
SHA-256 hash in `src/game/historicalAssetManifest.js`. Run
`npm run validate-assets` to verify the local release files.

The `dixie_instrumental.mp3` recording is included as separately licensed
CC BY-SA 4.0 Wikimedia Commons media; it is not relicensed under the project's
MIT code license. `lorena_instrumental.mp3` is included again using a trimmed
Library of Congress Citizen DJ source file whose item page states that the
recording is free to use and reuse, including commercial use. Optional recordings
and maps marked
`recording-needed` or `needs-review` are also excluded until their exact source
and redistribution status are verified.

## Copyrightability Note

The U.S. Copyright Office states that copyright protects human-authored
expression, while purely AI-generated material or material controlled only by
prompts may not be protected. See its
[Copyright and Artificial Intelligence initiative](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/)
and Part 2 report on copyrightability. Human-authored selection, arrangement,
editing, captions, software, and game design remain separate works.

### Historical landmark sprites — 2026-09-13

Henry House, Hackwood and Belle Grove PNGs in `public/images/battles/landmarks-v1/` were generated with the built-in OpenAI image tool for this game and retain their original RGBA pixels. These are illustrative reconstructions, not archival photographs. Henry House uses an NPS 1861 drawing as reference; Hackwood uses the house illustration in the user-provided interpretive-sign photograph; Belle Grove uses published architectural descriptions. Reference photographs are not included in the game bundle. Exact prompts, sources, limitations and hashes are in `docs/historical-landmark-prompts-v1.json` and `docs/historical-landmark-art-v1.json`.

### Historical landmark sprites, second batch — 2026-09-13

Shiloh Church, Chancellor House and Brotherton Cabin PNGs in `public/images/battles/landmarks-v2/` were generated with the built-in OpenAI image tool and retain their original RGBA pixels. No exact model identifier was reported. Shiloh uses the park's circa-1901 historical painting as interpretive reference; Chancellor House uses Forbes's May 1, 1863 drawing and NPS history; Brotherton uses NPS historical and architectural descriptions. ## September 13, 2026 — Wilderness and Cold Harbor landmarks (0.7.0)

`public/images/battles/landmarks-v4/widow-tapp-house.png` and `garthright-house.png` are original AI-generated illustrative reconstructions made with the built-in image generation tool. The tool did not report its exact model identifier. Original full-resolution RGBA output is retained without pixel editing. Prompts are in `docs/historical-landmark-prompts-v4.json`; source URLs, original paths, dimensions, transparency checks and SHA-256 hashes are in `docs/historical-landmark-art-v4.json`.

The houses and their historical roles are documented; exact architectural details, palette, scale and placement are interpretive. The Tapp log-house form follows the NPS wayside transcription. Garthright’s mixed brick and timber construction follows the NPS historic structures report and historical photograph. Historical photographs and the PDF are research evidence outside the release, not shipped textures. No claim is made that the generated images are archival photographs or surveyed reconstructions. Garthright hospital care adds historical context only, not a healing mechanic.

## September 13, 2026 — Second landmarks at Manassas and Chickamauga (0.10.0)

Stone House and Snodgrass House in `public/images/battles/landmarks-v5/` were generated with the built-in image tool. The exact model was not reported. Original RGBA files are retained without pixel edits. Their native alpha was checked; a proposed Snodgrass cleanup with a baked checkerboard was rejected. The Stone House follows the NPS-hosted 1862 photograph; Snodgrass follows an Alfred Waud drawing in the Library of Congress, whose exact date is uncertain. These are illustrative reconstructions, with representative placement and invented farm-track/yard detail. The existing Snodgrass cabin is a smaller 1867 rebuild. Historical reference images remain outside the game bundle. Prompts and source/hash records are in `docs/historical-landmark-prompts-v5.json` and `docs/historical-landmark-art-v5.json`.
