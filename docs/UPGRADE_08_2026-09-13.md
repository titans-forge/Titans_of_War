# Civil War upgrade 08 — Second battlefield landmarks

Release 0.10.0 · September 13, 2026 · battle rules 13 · campaign save envelope 17.

## What changed

First Manassas now has the Stone House as well as Henry House. The new stone tavern/farmhouse sprite follows the 1862 photograph hosted by the National Park Service. A farm approach connects its yard toward the Henry Hill surroundings, with a small cultivated patch to distinguish the household ground.

Chickamauga now has Snodgrass House near Horseshoe Ridge as well as Brotherton Cabin. Its longer log-house silhouette is informed by Alfred Waud's drawing in the Library of Congress. A farm track connects the yard toward the existing north–south road. The guide explains that the smaller surviving Snodgrass cabin is an 1867 rebuild; the drawing's precise date is uncertain.

The houses block movement and direct fire for both armies through the existing landmark system. Farm tracks, garden furrows and worn yards are illustrative scenery, with no additional cover, healing or movement bonus. Locations and scale are representative on compressed tactical boards, not surveyed coordinates.

All historical landmark notes now include a View button. It pauses play and centers the camera on the chosen building, closes the phone details drawer, and keeps the selected formation unchanged. Camera focus survives the resulting field resize. The phone toolbar also now checks its width when subscribing to resize events, covering a resize between the first render and that subscription.

## Historical and art evidence

- [NPS Stone House history](https://www.nps.gov/mana/learn/historyculture/the-stone-house.htm): crossroads location, wartime use and 1862 photograph. The photograph was inspected.
- [Library of Congress Waud drawing](https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/2004660147/): Snodgrass House appearance and uncertainty about the drawing's date. The drawing was inspected.
- [NPS historic resource inventory](https://www.nps.gov/parkhistory/online_books/chch/hrs/appendixb.pdf): the 1867 cabin reused original logs from a larger structure.

Both sprites were made with the built-in image generator. The tool did not report its exact model identifier. Original RGBA files are installed without pixel edits, with transparency and hashes checked. A cleanup attempt produced an RGB checkerboard and was rejected; another variant changed chimney character and was not used. Inspection confirmed the original Snodgrass background pixels are transparent and the sprite blends correctly on the field.

Assets: `public/images/battles/landmarks-v5/stone-house.png` and `snodgrass-house.png`. Prompts and provenance: `docs/historical-landmark-prompts-v5.json` and `docs/historical-landmark-art-v5.json`. Research images are retained outside the release.

## Verification and corrections

The full validation suite passes 852 named test executions, plus existing content, asset and agent-manifest checks. The final 26 landmark tests, 12 camera/input tests, 7 lifecycle tests and production build pass. Exact counts, command logs, screenshots and installation hashes are retained in the external evidence folder.

The final stress matrix covers 264 cases: eleven battles, four opening choices, three enemy doctrines and two readiness levels. No geometry/integrity flags and no changed outcome categories were found. Fifteen records differ within Manassas and Chickamauga; maximum Confederate loss-rate difference is 3.0844 percentage points. The other nine battles match exactly. These fixed-order cases are not human win-rate estimates.

A separate 264-case rules-12 matrix matches the original 0.9.0 baseline exactly. New campaigns and quick battles use rules 13; older saves retain their rules. Envelope 16 rejects rules 13, while envelope 17 round-trips both current rules and older archives. Save, result-ledger, campaign assessment and manual mechanics-upgrade entry points recognize the new version.

The first Stone House placement worsened all six passive-hold stress cases from defeat to rout and a passive browser run also ended in a rout. That placement was rejected. Moving the house farther toward the Matthews approach restored the prior outcome categories; the rejected matrix and initial screenshot remain in evidence. Initial route-test endpoints also needed correction: they overlapped Henry House or lay beyond the northern board edge. Final tests start and end on clear, legal ground and check every traversed segment against all buildings.

Browser checks cover both houses, source notes, occupied-ground overlays, desktop overview and phone View navigation at 390×844. Native assets blend without the rejected checkerboard. Physical-device performance and extended campaign continuity remain separate follow-up work. Existing large-bundle build warnings remain.
