# Upgrade 24 — a current-rule Manassas counterattack reference

Release 0.26.0. Campaign envelope 21 and battle rules 17 remain unchanged.

## What changed

The former reference sent Jackson and Elzey through the same narrow western approach beside Henry House. Under current rules they could obstruct one another and fail to secure the battery line. The new reference stages Jackson at 1030,610 and sends Elzey around to 700,230. Bee and Hampton retain their supporting positions; the guns remain on Hold. The player still selects formations, sets Line, previews and confirms moves, and issues both assaults.

Current Manassas attack missions now expose all four plan positions in Find on field. A View staging position button locates the selected point without issuing an order. Step labels distinguish a reserve that has not arrived, movement in progress, reaching the approach and an assault commitment. The revised reference is used for battle rules 17 onward; earlier rules keep their existing reference. Defense and withdrawal missions receive no attack-plan markers.

The positions are a representative gameplay reference, not a reconstruction of historical orders. No building collision, turning clearance, weapon, morale, deadline or victory requirement changed. Congestion recovery remains an explicit player decision.

## Browser playthrough

A full-ammunition, Reserve counterstroke, option A encounter was played through the production UI. Staging moves were previewed and confirmed; Elzey received his move around minute 52, Jackson attacked around minute 62, and Elzey attacked after the UI showed At approach around minute 105. The result was **Decisive field victory, command 97, Confederate losses 13%**, with two supporting infantry and no organized defenders. The practice report retained the selected condition and objective result; the campaign remained unchanged. This live timing differs from the fixed script, providing one additional manual example rather than a general novice-success claim.

Phone-size 390×844 inspection confirmed that the future reserve approach can be found before Elzey arrives and that viewing it preserves paused time and unit selection. No errors were returned by the tab log query. Physical devices and new-player studies remain open.
