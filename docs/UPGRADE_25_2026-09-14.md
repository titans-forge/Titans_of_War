# Upgrade 25 — Shiloh attack timing and battlefield orientation

Release 0.27.0. Battle rules 17 and campaign envelope 21 are unchanged.

## Player-facing improvements

The current Shiloh attack brief now explains when to review the assault: around field minutes 20–22, while the opening march is still in progress. Previously it gave staging destinations without a commitment cue. Waiting for every corps to reach those destinations can leave the attack delayed and congested as the Union reserve moves and Buell arrives.

The revised brief explains how to stage all four corps in Line, issue their assaults while paused, preserve the required eight-minute supported hold and respond to blocked turning space. The existing staging positions are retained: moving Breckinridge to several alternate routes did not consistently improve results. These are playable-board references, not historical orders.

Both Shiloh attack choices now expose all four staging positions in Find on field, plus View staging position beside the selected step. Viewing a position pauses and marks the map without changing the selected formation or issuing orders. Earlier battle rules retain their previous brief; reserve-defense and withdrawal missions receive no attack-plan markers.

## Fixed-order checks

The final reproducible comparison uses the actual Shiloh practice scenario, current rules, both attack choices, all three Union doctrines and all three ammunition presets. The previous-reference group uses commitments at 25, 35 and 45; 25 is the legacy test timing, while 35 and 45 examine further delay. The former UI did not prescribe those times. The revised group uses 20, 21 and 22. All other player orders and positions are identical.

| Starting ammunition | Later commitments: objective achieved | Earlier review window: objective achieved |
|---|---:|---:|
| Full | 9/18 | 15/18 |
| Limited | 8/18 | 15/18 |
| Depleted | 7/18 | 14/18 |

This is 108 deterministic cases, not human win rates. Mean full-supply losses decreased from 14.2% to 5.7%. Timing remains sensitive: the full-supply cases at 20 and 22 all succeeded, but only three of six at 21 did. The guidance therefore describes a review window and recovery decisions, not a guaranteed sequence. Failed cases and unsuccessful alternate staging experiments are retained. No deadlines, combat values, collision rules, ammunition or victory requirements were changed.

## Production-UI playthrough

Full ammunition, Reserve counterstroke, option A was played through the normal UI. Four staging moves were individually previewed and confirmed. The assault was committed at approximately minute 26, later than the reference window. At minute 69, Army attention identified Polk blocked by Bragg. Preview room to turn offered a destination at 1155,787; it was confirmed. At minute 90 Polk had finished that move and received Assault again.

The battle then achieved the objective with two supporting infantry and no organized defenders. The practice report recorded **Decisive Confederate victory, command 98/100, Confederate losses 10%, Union losses 28%**. Hardee routed during the encounter; the report correctly advised earlier recovery. This is one manually adapted victory, not evidence that every late attack or clearance move wins. The campaign remained unchanged.
