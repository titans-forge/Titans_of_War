# Gettysburg: Abraham Brian farm and connected surroundings — 0.19.0

Gettysburg now pairs Abraham Brian House with Leister House. Five boards have two named historical structures. New native farmhouse art, cultivated patches and worn access tracks make this sector read as inhabited farm ground. Both landmarks use the existing View controls on desktop and phone.

The farmhouse is an obstacle to movement and direct fire for both armies. Surroundings remain decorative, with no cover, speed, healing or garrison bonus. Existing Gettysburg ridge, wall, road, sectors and withdrawal rules are retained. New campaigns and quick battles use tactical rules 17; save envelope 21 supports the new provenance. Older campaigns keep their own rules unless the player selects the existing per-battle upgrade control.

## Historical basis and limits

The [NPS Abraham Brian Farm history](https://www.nps.gov/places/abraham-brian-farm.htm) documents the farm, its crops and fencing, the African American family's flight before the invasion, and losses on their return. The [NPS house tour](https://home.nps.gov/gett/learn/photosmultimedia/abraham-brian-house-3d.htm) locates it near the northern end of Cemetery Ridge. The current NPS exterior photograph was visually inspected: small whitewashed dwelling, shingled gable roof, shallow front pent eave, central door and paired sash windows. The game's roof and siding finishes are illustrative, not verified 1863 restoration details. The 3D interior tour was not inspected.

The [NPS Leister then-and-now account](https://www.nps.gov/gett/learn/photosmultimedia/buildings-and-farms-then-and-now.htm) supports the headquarters and surrounding farm setting. Tracks and cultivated plots are compositional connections on the compressed battlefield, not surveyed historic road traces. They meet the existing road visually, without changing its crossing penalty. No new fences imply additional defensive cover. The NPS record of Brian's damaged barn is not sufficient architectural evidence to add a specific barn reconstruction in this pass.

The original 1536 × 1024 RGBA farmhouse sprite was copied unchanged, with actual alpha from 0 to 254. See [art provenance](historical-landmark-art-v7.json) and [generation prompt](historical-landmark-prompts-v7.json). Asset size: 3,106,284 bytes.
