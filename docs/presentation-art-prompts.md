# Artwork generation record

Built-in `image_gen` was used for all three assets. No local Flux/Bonsai job was started. The tool returned 1672×941 PNG masters despite the larger dimensions requested in the prompts. Originals are preserved in this folder. Runtime WebP copies retain the full image dimensions; quality87 encoding is the only processing. Actual hashes, paths and byte sizes are in Asset-Manifest.json. All images are new interpretations and require specialist review before claims of equipment or architectural accuracy.

## Integration and source checks

Scene/Archive/Map controls keep illustration separate from the existing archive and historical maps. Actual runtime art uses its full native aspect ratio at every viewport; no square crop was needed. The new images are not used as tactical terrain or historical source documents.

- [NPS Fort Sumter account](https://home.nps.gov/articles/battle-of-fort-sumter-april-1861.htm) supports April13 acceptance of terms and April14 evacuation, not the specific depicted figures or viewpoint.
- [NOAA Monitor history](https://monitor.noaa.gov/shipwrecks/uss_monitor.html) supports the March9 ironclad encounter. The game's March8 decision explicitly labels the image as following-day context.
- [NPS surrender documents](https://home.nps.gov/apco/learn/historyculture/surrender-documents.htm) supports the terms and paroles. The illustration does not impersonate a photograph of Grant and Lee or imply every Confederate army surrendered at Appomattox.

Preserved limitations: uniforms/insignia, the gun carriage/barrel orientation, ship dimensions, and village architecture have not received a historian or equipment-specialist sign-off. The generated masters are adequate for the tested game slots but below the larger production-master target.
