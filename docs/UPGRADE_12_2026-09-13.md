# Civil War upgrade 12 — Optional withdrawal goals

Release 0.14.0 continues phase 1 of the highest-value upgrade sequence.

## Player-facing change

Ten withdrawal choices across First Manassas, Shiloh, the Wilderness, Third Winchester, Cedar Creek and Gettysburg now show optional replay goals: withdraw 90% of starting strength in order, bring every formation out in order, and recover every artillery formation where guns are present. Live progress is collapsed by default in formation details. Completed goals appear in the battle report and are retained with campaign assessment records in the journal.

These are observations, not new victory conditions or rewards. A normal successful withdrawal remains successful. Goals require mission success; routing and surrendered formations do not count as orderly exits, scheduled arrivals remain in the denominator, and abandoned guns cannot earn gun recovery. Battles end at the existing mission resolution, so departures must be planned together. Delegation, transfer missions and snapshots without sufficient observations do not receive goals. Historical artwork is unchanged in this gameplay pass.

## Validation

- Full `npm run validate`: 901 passing named test executions, zero failures. Includes nine new withdrawal-goal tests covering thresholds, disorder, scheduled arrivals, gun abandonment, unsupported observations, malformed records and an actual Gettysburg result through campaign save/restore.
- Production build passed.
- Ten real withdrawal simulations compared live and completed assessments to installed 0.13.0: all existing assessment fields match exactly after removing the optional goal annotation. Combat implementation files are unchanged. This is assessment parity, not a new full balance audit.
- In-app browser: played Gettysburg B by ordering all five formations to withdraw, then running at 4x. All five escaped in order with zero losses; command score 100 and goals 3/3. Inspected live desktop goals, the completed desktop report and a 390 x 844 report, including scrolling to gun recovery. Browser error log empty. Phone coverage is an emulated viewport, not a physical device.
- Save retention was exercised by an engine-backed automated archive/restore test; this pass did not complete a live campaign route.

## Compatibility and remaining work

Battle rules stay 15 and campaign envelope stays 19. The optional version-1 assessment annotation is additive; old assessments without it remain valid. No dependencies, combat rules, terrain, assets, score weights or campaign rewards change.

The one-shot immediate-withdraw policy succeeded with all applicable goals in seven of ten cases. It still routed at the Wilderness and failed both Winchester withdrawals. Those observations are retained in `evidence/grading-parity.json`; they are not evidence that those missions are impossible. Next: play those missions with their intended timing and rearguard requirements and resolve any demonstrated briefing or command issues. Mission depth remains in progress before the next connected-battlefield art phase.
