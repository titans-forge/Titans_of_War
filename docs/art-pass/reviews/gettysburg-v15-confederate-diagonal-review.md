# Gettysburg v15 Confederate diagonal art

This local opt-in pass adds Confederate A southeast and southwest views for walking, ready carry, firing and two loading states. Gettysburg is the pilot. Older art policies, battle rules 18 and campaign save envelope 22 remain in place. Use `?artPass=gettysburg-v15` to inspect the new local art; default-art promotion and public release are outside this pass.

## Selection and registration

The built-in image-generation tool produced the new RGBA source sheets. Its underlying model ID was not exposed. New native Blender guides supplied actual diagonal camera views and alternating complete leg chains. Two direct walking attempts in each bearing repeated supporting legs and were rejected. The selected walking sources followed color-coded realistic intermediates and a further built-in material edit; the final sources require fresh inspection and measurements. The first action sheets failed cell padding and were rejected; the replacement action sheets have more room around complete weapons and boots.

Source reviews inspect the complete four-cell sheet, actual bearing, kit, leg-chain roles or action grips, cap regions, supporting-sole regions and visible firing barrel mouth. Registration uses per-cell pelvis-center X projected to the estimated supporting sole Y, with one median cap-to-sole body height for the whole source. These are approximate 2D registration measurements. Atlas authoring copies each selected PNG byte for byte, retaining its native alpha, dimensions and four equal cells.

The source annotations bind exact source and independent model review hashes. Four new atlas pairs provide eight new clips, retaining all 32 v14 atlas pairs and 64 clips. Native Confederate A preview coverage becomes all eight directions. Missing sources, whole actions or other identities retain the existing accepted fallback. Older passes retain their previous poses and clip addresses.

## Acceptance evidence

All four selected source, public atlas and production-build PNG copies are byte-identical. The four native 1254 × 1254 RGBA sheets add 23.9947 MiB of nominal texture pixels and 1,927,822 bytes of runtime PNG storage, within the 32 MiB increment budget. The selected v15 set totals 36 atlases, 72 clips and 215.9522 MiB of nominal pixels. This excludes other field textures: the browser diagnostic reported 106 total field textures and 389.73 MiB nominal RGBA. Neither value measures GPU or process residency.

Verification passed: 1,292 gameplay regression checks; 160 current art checks, including 11 new real-native scene/Phaser checks; 294 asset checks in total; the unchanged historical native authors plus the new v15 author; 370 malformed real-manifest/review/native-source rejection cases; and the production build. The fixture was captured from the original and independently recaptured: 32 old atlas pairs, 64 clip addresses, 12 previous policies, 71,168 previous-pose samples, 165 old review rows and 112 old presets retain their expected values. The fixture and extracted-scene checks are software evidence, not human playtesting.

Browser review inspected all four marching phases, the ready/fire/load/ram sequence, synchronized bearings, running playback and pause. A bounded Gettysburg practice test ordered Pickett southeast and southwest, inspected the moving and stopped formations, and cold-loaded a paused save at field time 14 with matching visible strength, ammunition, order, front and support status. The practice was discarded afterward; the candidate campaign remained on turn 1 with unchanged resources. No browser warnings or errors were observed. This art pass did not repeat a complete encounter, verify firing in a live diagonal combat situation, or establish named-PC performance; real scene volleys and diagonal checkpoint continuation were checked by the native suite.

Scoped installation, rollback and post-install receipts belong in the external work folder `Titans_of_War_Art_Work/gettysburg-v15-20261003`. They identify the exact installed files and evidence; this document does not independently establish that an upload or default promotion happened.

Measured walking spans are 417/398/417/407 pixels for SE (median 412, range 19) and 411/392/380/364 for SW (median 386, range 47). SW cap tops vary by 6 pixels while selected supporting soles vary by 52. Most measured variation is at the lower boundary, but these 2D observations cannot separate foot depth, posture, framing or anatomical drift. Registration deliberately uses one source-wide height and retains that variation. The first SE passing foot projects below its interpreted farther support, and the final SW passing lift is modest; those depth/contact interpretations remain uncertain.

## Remaining priorities

1. Improve walking consistency, ready-to-aim and loading transitions, shoulder attachment and recoil foot contact. Address SW apparent height variation and ambiguous passing-foot depth first. Four discrete poses remain visibly less continuous than a rigged animation.
2. Add staff-gripping standard bearers, artillery crews, close-combat/contact and casualty poses; keep those roles separate from marching riflemen.
3. Finish missing Union A/B bearings and additional Confederate identities with consistent kit and camera measurements.
4. Optimize the growing texture set and run complete encounters on named PCs, followed by informed human history and visual review, before default promotion and the next battlefield.

Model-assisted source review and software/browser checks support this limited preview. They do not certify anatomy, historical drill, human usability, a 10/10 grade or device performance.
