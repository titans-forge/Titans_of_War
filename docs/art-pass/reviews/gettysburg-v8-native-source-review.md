# Gettysburg v8 native Union SW source review

Date: 2026-10-02. Scope: the four explicitly named native Union identity-A/B SW march and action sheets in this isolated candidate checkout. This is a separate model-assisted, read-only source inspection. It supports limited opt-in source previews under the existing 16 px solid-padding gate. Production, full-family, human, anatomical, historical and exact-drill certification remain false. Runtime registration, animation transitions, actual effect placement and board-scale player quality are outside this source review.

The reviewer viewed the complete native images with `view_image`. Pixel measurements read the supplied PNGs without writing altered images. No source was cropped, cleaned, resampled, repacked or rearranged on disk by this review. Temporary in-memory pixel windows were used only for read-only measurement. Generation/edit requests do not establish that a requested percentage, exact size, face-only change or pose preservation was achieved; the observations and measurements below describe the resulting files.

The registered `union-sw-march-guide.png` and `union-sw-actions-guide.png` were viewed as structural references. The v7 A/B S march sources were also viewed for identity, equipment and gait comparison. The primitive guides are not anatomical or historical oracles. Their phase labels and carrying side are not automatically inherited by a generated source.

Both final march sheets were traced afresh from hip through thigh, knee, lower leg and boot. Foot ordering alone was not used to label anatomy. The two contacts and the two passing supports plausibly oppose each other in each source:

| 3 | Anatomical left support passing | The viewer-right/left hip feeds the straighter supporting column and low boot. The anatomical-right chain bends into the lifted knee and higher boot. |

The observed chronological sequence for each march is **native cell 0 → cell 3 → cell 2 → cell 1 → cell 0**, represented as `[0,3,2,1]`. The generated passing cells reverse the guide's native top-right/bottom-right phase assignment. The source pixels must retain their native placement; source-to-phase metadata should express the observed sequence. This is a plausible four-picture gait, not proof of continuous skeletal motion, ground reaction, planted-foot trajectories or exact marching drill.

All four cells in both sheets read as an elevated three-quarter SW/down-left bearing through the face, torso, thigh projection and toe direction. The camera/body bearing is shallower than the primitive SW guide in some cells. The long firearm stays toward viewer-right, with the anatomical-left upper fore-end hand and anatomical-right lower stock hand visibly attached through the phases. The guide instead places upright carry toward viewer-left. The final source has a coherent chosen alternative carrying pose; exact historical posture and equipment dimensions remain unverified.

## Identity, uniform and equipment observations

The A march and action sources retain a bearded, dark-haired adult face, navy cap and coat, pale blue trousers, brown boots, crossing straps, belt/pouch regions, a disk canteen and a horizontal bedroll/pack arrangement. The final A ready pose now uses the march's viewer-right upright carry. The final A loading cell has one upright loading weapon; the extra diagonal rifle seen in its prior carry attempt is absent in the fresh final view.

The B sources show a more conspicuous moustache and a trimmed/lighter chin region relative to A. This is plausibly consistent across B's downward-looking views, while remaining a subtle distinction. It does not establish the requested older age, an exact likeness to a legacy B face or strong identity differentiation at game scale. B retains the same major uniform and kit relationships. Its action edit also enlarged the body and firearm relative to A and its own preceding reference; an exact face-only edit or unchanged scale must not be claimed.

These observations establish recognizable project-art continuity at source scale. They do not establish a complete campaign-kit inventory, verified firearm model, regiment-specific clothing, exact fingers or immutable rig geometry.
