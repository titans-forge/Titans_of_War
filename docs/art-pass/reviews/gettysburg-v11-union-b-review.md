# Gettysburg v11: Union B east and southeast

This local opt-in pass gives the second Union infantry identity its own east and southeast march, ready, fire, load and ram poses. Four native RGBA sheets add sixteen source frames and eight clips. The selected pass now contains 26 atlases and 52 clips, including the exact retained v10 set of 22 atlases and 44 clips.

Use `?artPass=gettysburg-v11` for historical Gettysburg. The close-up development viewer at `?artReview=gettysburg-motion` offers **V11 B E ↔ SE march**, **V11 B E ↔ SE action sequence**, turn inspection and A/B comparisons. This remains a partial family for local visual review.

## Visible changes

- B keeps its brown-haired, moustached appearance, navy wool cap and coat, pale blue trousers, brown brogans, back-mounted knapsack and blanket roll. Pouch/canteen relationships were checked relative to the body across each source.
- Marching uses four distinct contact/passing roles with opposite supporting legs. Ordinary generated attempts repeated a support pose; those sources were withheld. Native Blender guides with blue anatomical-left and red anatomical-right legs made the roles inspectable before a fresh generated uniform conversion.
- Stationary poses include a shouldered firing silhouette and two different representative loading jobs. Firing anchors refer to the approximate barrel tube mouth, separately from the extended bayonet tip.
- Every final PNG retains the builtin output bytes. Atlas registration uses complete equal cells and one common body reference per source. No raster mirroring, recoloring, resampling, crop or alpha cleanup was applied.

## Limits and remaining priorities

1. Continue exact side/identity/bearing coverage. Union A/B now have E,SE,S,SW,W; remaining Union northern bearings and Confederate identity/view coverage still need authored poses. Confederate A N,NE,NW remain retained. Missing combinations use the existing explicit fallback policy.
2. Add recoil and settling, aim transitions, contact/casualty reactions and dedicated standard-bearer handling. One held fire pose and two loading poses are not a complete verified drill.
3. Test continuity and historical kit with independent human play and a knowledgeable reviewer. Model-assisted visual agreement cannot certify physical anatomy, hidden equipment attachments, exact 3D yaw or historical drill.
4. Establish matched active-battle performance evidence and a texture/residency strategy before extending the full family or promoting it as default.
5. Investigate the displayed command-delay rounding/refresh difference and restore camera/inspector preferences as separate convenience work.

Fresh source roots, cap measurements, one-reference scaling and uncertainties are bound in the four-source annotation manifest and separate exact-SHA reviews.
