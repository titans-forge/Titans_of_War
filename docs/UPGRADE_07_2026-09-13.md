# Civil War upgrade 07 — Mobile battlefield command

Release 0.9.0 · September 13, 2026 · battle rules 12 · campaign save envelope 16.

## What changed

Choosing an active formation in either roster now centers the camera on that unit. Phone focus uses at least 0.8 world zoom, making the representative ranks appreciably larger than the previous twice-overview view. A closer manual zoom is preserved. Selecting an unarrived reserve still shows its details without moving the camera to an off-field position.

The camera toolbar labels Overview, Focus, Pan and Move. All six primary controls and the Map & sound menu fit in one row at 320 pixels, with targets at least 44 pixels wide and tall. The collapsed formation summary includes troop strength beside condition, cohesion and ammunition. The existing detail drawer pauses play and keeps its underlying field inert.

Overlapping formation bodies now resolve to the nearest active formation using actual unit positions, rather than draw order or the polygon object's origin. An explicit standard/flag hit takes precedence. Friendly ranks remain selectable under enemy overlap. One-finger dragging still pans; a second simultaneous contact cancels the gesture so releasing it cannot leave a movement or group preview. Pinch-to-zoom is not added: use the visible zoom buttons.

Battle simulation, campaign storage, terrain, historical art, reinforcement timing and balance files are byte-identical to 0.8.0. This is a control and presentation release.
