# Civil War upgrade 13 — Clear withdrawal command

Release 0.15.0 completes the targeted Winchester and Wilderness withdrawal playtests within phase 1.

## Findings and changes

Wilderness D is achievable without losses when orders account for the Texas Brigade and Longstreet. The opening-only withdrawal policy leaves them holding after arrival and loses the mission. Its previous quick-battle description and command-brief subtitle still emphasized waiting and counterattacking, despite the selected withdrawal strategy.

The Wilderness withdrawal now has matching selected-strategy guidance in quick battle, the campaign battle gate and the field brief. Above the battlefield, a compact checkpoint shows organized exits against 65% of the entire starting army and each reserve's arrival, blocked deployment, need for orders, withdrawal or departure status. The brief identifies the scheduled minutes 12 and 20 and explains that new arrivals need their own orders. Failed withdrawal reports offer that specific replay advice.

Winchester B/D already enforce earned cover before a successful corridor exit. The updated selected brief and always-visible checkpoints show Middle Field 18 minutes or rearguard 8 minutes, followed by 65% organized corridor escape and an orderly exit by Gordon or Wharton. The heading changes from cover needed to bring the screen home. These are existing mission requirements, distinct from the optional withdrawal goals.

This pass changes presentation and replay advice only. Battle rules remain 15, save envelope 19. No combat implementation, deployments, reward weights, assets or dependencies change. Earlier tactical rules below 4 keep their original brief paths.
