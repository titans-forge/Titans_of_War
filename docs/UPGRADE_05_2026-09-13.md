# Civil War upgrade 05 — Wilderness and Cold Harbor

Release 0.7.0 · September 13, 2026 · battle rules 11 · campaign save envelope 15.

## Delivered

- **The Wilderness:** Widow Tapp House is a new physical landmark with original generated log-house art. A connected farm lane reaches the Orange Plank Road. Tapp’s clearing removes woodland cover and increases movement to open-ground pace; its geometry also controls tree exclusion and the optional terrain overlay. The battle’s existing visibility limits remain in force.
- **Cold Harbor:** Garthright House is a new physical landmark behind the Union opening line, with generated brick-and-timber art and a sourced explanation of its field-hospital history. Confederate works, assault ground and the Petersburg transfer routes retain their rules.
- **Movement reliability:** fixed an infantry corner stall discovered beside Garthright House. New rules check route segments from the formation’s actual position and retain corner waypoints until the next segment is clear. All sides still obey building footprints for movement, direct fire and close combat.
- **Campaign compatibility:** new games and quick battles use rules 11. Older saves retain their recorded mechanics; envelope 14 still rejects rules 11. The existing per-battle opt-in now offers the current rules.

There are now dedicated physical landmark buildings on ten of eleven playable scenarios. Petersburg already has the Crater, so every playable battlefield has at least one recognizable historical landmark. This completes first-landmark coverage, not the broader goal of one or two fully developed historical sites on every battlefield.

## Historical grounding and art

[Tapp Field, National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/places/tapp-field.htm) documents the rented farm, the civilian family and the crisis before Longstreet’s arrival. The [photographed NPS wayside transcription](https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=112326) describes a modest 1.5-story log house. The house sprite is an illustrative reconstruction; precise window placement, chimney treatment and materials are not measured reconstructions.

[Cold Harbor battlefield tour, NPS](https://www.nps.gov/rich/learn/historyculture/cold-harbor-tour.htm) places Garthright behind Wright’s VI Corps after June 3 and records ten days of hospital use. The [NPS historical photograph](https://www.nps.gov/rich/planyourvisit/placestogo.htm) and [NPS Historic Structures Report](https://npshistory.com/publications/rich/garthright-house-hsr.pdf) inform the mixed brick/frame construction, plain center-hall form, end chimneys and partial basement. The art is interpretive, and hospital history does not create a healing or garrison bonus.

Both sprites were created with the built-in image generation tool. Original full-resolution RGBA images are copied unchanged into `public/images/battles/landmarks-v4/`; the exact model identifier was not reported. See `historical-landmark-prompts-v4.json` for prompts and `historical-landmark-art-v4.json` for provenance and hashes. Research photos/PDFs remain outside the shipped game.

## Validation

- `npm run validate`: **802 named tests passed**, plus the existing content, agent-manifest and asset checks.
- **100 landmark tests** across four batches. The 28 new checks cover save boundaries, all openings/doctrines, initial formations and arrivals, infantry/artillery routing in all four directions, blocked orders, direct fire, close combat, formation widening, road clearance, cover/movement semantics, actual reinforcement arrivals, transfer lanes and fixed-step determinism.
- Production build passed. The final flat farm-lane metadata change was followed by another build and all 100 landmark tests.
- **264 rules-11 stress cases and 264 legacy rules-10 cases**, all with zero flagged geometry/outcome-integrity issues. Rules-10 results exactly match the prior installed release’s saved results.
- New rules change 21 detailed records across Manassas, Wilderness, Cedar Creek and alternate Gettysburg. **No outcome categories changed**. The largest per-case casualty difference is 0.186 percentage points; Cold Harbor’s 24 records remain identical. This fixed-order stress matrix is not a human win-rate estimate.
- Browser checks: both houses render without backgrounds and have readable desktop labels and source notes; invalid destinations disable confirmation; a clear alternative enables it. The Wilderness was run through Texas Brigade and Longstreet arrivals. Cold Harbor’s opening assault was observed. Phone layout and the landmark inspector were checked at 390×844.

Initial failed tests are retained in external evidence. They exposed the real corner stall and insufficient Tapp road clearance; two test fixtures also needed correction (the commitment property name and isolating arrival checks from automatic empty-battle adjudication). The road was given sufficient turning clearance and production routing was repaired before passing validation.

## Scope and remaining work

This is a local installation with a frozen hash manifest and rollback copies. No itch.io upload, commit or push is part of this batch. Browser sessions covered opening phases, not every battle to a human-played victory. The full-board phone view still makes tiny labels and soldiers difficult to read; zoom/focus and the details drawer help but do not constitute a complete mobile quality pass.

Next highest-value work:

1. Make terrain differences more readable, especially Cold Harbor’s broken assault ground and interlocking defenses, with visuals aligned to movement and cover rules.
2. Add a second grounded site where it improves the battle: Petersburg’s salient/covered-way context, followed by supporting landmarks and connected ground on the other boards. Research wartime forms and avoid modern monuments.
3. Improve phone camera, selection and formation inspection through complete battle sessions; then run full campaign-to-save-to-return playtests before preparing a Civil War release package.
