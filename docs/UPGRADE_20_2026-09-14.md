# Upgrade 20 — campaign command continuity

Release: 0.22.0. Campaign save version 21 and battle rules 17 remain unchanged.

## Changes

After-action reports now name the outgoing commander and role, the next commander and role, the next encounter and its date. Changed command perspectives are explained explicitly: campaign resources and recorded decisions carry forward, while the player is not transferring the same troops from one army to another.

The handoff also reports unpaid Treasury and Ammunition obligations and remaining emergency reserves. Accounts with debt open automatically. Before an authored field encounter, it shows the current ammunition allocation using the existing supply formula, and explains that supply planning can change it before commitment. Endings instead show final obligations and no next assignment. Legacy supply rules do not acquire invented obligation records.

New journal entries include the commander and role. The handoff is part of the saved after-action report; it survives reload without applying consequences again. Older reports remain readable. Malformed optional handoffs are rejected safely while preserving campaign state.
