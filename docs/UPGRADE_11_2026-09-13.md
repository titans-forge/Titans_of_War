# Civil War Upgrade 11 — Chickamauga preservation (0.13.0)

Chickamauga C and D now use a consistent preservation standard for new battle rules15: retain at least65% of starting strength in organized on-field formations or troops withdrawn in order. Routed/captured troops and not-yet-arrived reserves cannot satisfy it. Holding to126, a favorable field result, and a completed orderly exit are valid routes; no attack or casualties are required.

## Why this changed

The previous mission labels both promised preservation, but passive C failed while passive D succeeded with the same intact army. C's staff estimate also used an attacking calculation. C now uses the same preservation outcome thresholds as D, while retaining its own readiness modifiers and campaign choice consequences. The coordination delay and rail operation remain strategic consequences, not invented tactical bridge or cabinet objectives.

The battle-entry briefing, Quick Battle description, arrival notices, command subtitle, live retained-force readout and debrief explain the actual selected mission. Scores explicitly measure completion and preservation, not mission difficulty.
