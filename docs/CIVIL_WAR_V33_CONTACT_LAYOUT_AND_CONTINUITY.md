# Civil War v33: contact readability and continuity

This pass implements an optional Gettysburg display layout on the accepted v32 artwork. It makes opposing ranks easier to distinguish without changing combat positions, collision footprints, casualties, movement orders or volley origins. The local preview requires `?artPass=gettysburg-v32&infantryScale=moderate&contactLayout=v33`. Other queries and battlefields retain the existing presentation.

## Changes, in priority order

1. Give eligible isolated infantry contact pairs equal, opposite display offsets of at most three world pixels, approached at six pixels per simulation second. Living soldiers share a single vector so their internal rank spacing is preserved. Both formations must have exactly one actual close-combat neighbor and mutual melee state. Resolve the available distance against every living foot inside the actual oriented footprint using Gettysburg's uncompressed tactical ground coordinates.
2. Keep casualty positions continuous. A newly fallen soldier receives the preceding common display vector; that vector then stays attached to its exact raw fall tuple. Other live bodies can release independently. Inactive departing views retain their cached presentation.
3. Protect firing and saves. Recent actual volleys or a live firing pose immediately zero the living offsets on both participants. The native shot sampler receives the original raw visual. Save the optional layout as a bounded versioned sidecar and restore it only when its time, formation identities, capacities and fall tuples match the accepted base presentation. Preserve the first restored draw, including an immediate re-save before that draw. Invalid optional data falls back to zero offset while valid combat and base presentation remain usable. An independently reproduced numeric producer defect at arbitrary angles was repaired: computed vectors are canonicalized inside the exact three-pixel limit without relaxing validation of external records.
4. Register the new regression suite in full gameplay, current art and field continuity gates. Preserve old fixtures and assertions through an explicit six-file metadata migration, with authentic installed-v32 before archives and current-source behavior witnesses.

## Evidence and limits

The external prototype uses the actual Scene poses and real PNG/atlas pivots. Its final capture contains 106 Scene snapshots and 530 measured variants across eight bearings, four phases and three body profiles, with 56 real PNG/atlas bindings. In controlled moderate-profile cases, the common three-pixel offset reduced opposing alpha-mask intersection by 40.5 to 62.4 percent across the eight bearings. Within-army overlap stayed essentially unchanged within the sampling grid. Front-rank-only movement increased within-army overlap. Four pixels were not consistently better in every phase. All controlled three/four-pixel raw foot positions remained inside their footprints.

These are fixed-camera alpha-mask measurements and synthetic matched composites, not browser GPU performance or human readability certification. Dense rear-rank crowding remains. The before source is the authentic installed v32 Scene, also archived in the preservation fixture. The initial incorrect 0.72 coordinate-compression assumption and the first synthetic shadow-compositor images are retained with their corrections; neither is reported as a production defect or discarded.

Source smoke checks compare the disabled current Scene against that authentic before Scene and use the actual new helper for enabled contact and cold re-save checks. These are stub-Phaser state/position witnesses, not rendered-pixel proof. An independent actual-module sweep checked 3,600 arbitrary bearings with living and fallen own-checkpoint restores and immediate pre-draw re-saves; strict over-limit external offsets still reject. A separate current-Scene witness across nine controlled bearings checked that live cached departing bodies and offsets survive inactive draws/checkpoint-only sampling, with no expired-unit resurrection. Its controlled retirement and display recorder are not public retirement or GPU validation. Warm/cold inactive cached-image parity is not claimed; the same inherited mismatch is observed in authentic before-v32 and current disabled source witnesses. The final candidate targeted suite contains 31 passing cases, including all four contact phases across eight bearings, exact raw/shot/body invariants and malformed sidecar rejection. The art suite passed 627 executions, asset suite 761, save suite 67 and field continuity suite 92. The full gameplay pipeline passed 1,872 executions. ## Remaining priorities

1. Review this optional layout in ordinary play at overview and close zoom before any default promotion or wider battle rollout. Confirm volleys, asymmetric/multiple contacts, route departure and cold Resume remain understandable.
2. Reduce dense rank crowding through a separately reviewed formation-art scale/layout pass. Avoid claiming the present small offset solves overlap or proves historical drill.
3. Investigate cold standard/flag carrier continuity with matched saved cameras and before/current witnesses. The current Scene retains `view.standardAnchorId` between draws, and `gettysburgInfantryStandardAnchor` keeps that living figure; a fresh Scene chooses the nearest living foot again. A matched source witness reproduced carrier 16 changing to Pickett carrier 19 and Gibbon carrier 18 on cold restore identically in the authentic before-v32 Scene, current disabled Scene and current enabled Scene, with the same raw visible feet/fall tuples. That isolates an existing unsaved carrier-cache difference in the source witness; it does not establish full public-canvas identity or replay the browser command history exactly. Decide whether the bounded carrier ID should restore or settle visibly.
4. Expand close-combat pose diversity for the remaining bearings and identities only after readable ground spacing is settled; inspect complete pairs and casualty transitions at fixed cameras.
5. Obtain independent human play feedback for command clarity and visual readability, and measure browser frame timing on representative hardware before revising overall grades or promoting the experiment.

No native pixels, atlas annotations, combat/save owner version or default art policy change in this pass. This document does not establish a whole-game ten-out-of-ten grade or public release.
