## Acceptance and limits

The tool returned 1536×1024 infantry/crew sheets and a 1254×1254 aiming sheet. Frame rectangles follow actual cutouts, not the requested grid dimensions. Measured boot pivots and normalized figure height prevent animation frames from jumping. The native transparent backgrounds were verified from PNG alpha and in the game.

Two attempted whole-sheet directional edits were rejected: one clipped the firing figures' legs, both reduced photographic quality, and the intended rear aiming correction remained wrong. A separate front/back firing sheet resolved that issue. West and northeast use mirrored side/quarter views. These are representative uniforms and limited-frame 2D animation, not an exact uniform catalogue or motion capture. Reload and melee use the consistent figure artwork with procedural recovery/thrust; a future hand-authored limb animation pass can make those actions more detailed.
