## Player-facing changes

A compact opening menu offers Begin campaign or Continue campaign, Learn command, and Quick battle. The current chapter and commander identify the campaign being continued. Fresh campaigns can choose Story or Standard before entering the briefing. Existing campaigns retain a separate New campaign action using the existing checkpoint-protection confirmation. Completed campaigns offer their report.

Learn command is also directly available in the campaign header. Training opened from the menu returns to the menu; training opened from Quick battle returns to that library; training opened from the campaign header returns to the campaign. No campaign decision is committed by entering or leaving a drill.

Setup, challenge details and role explanations are grouped into one disclosure. Less-used help and reset controls remain in the command room and expanded campaign desk. The resulting campaign page brings the scene artwork and first decision cards higher into view. The walkthrough now describes the current field-save behavior instead of incorrectly saying battles must always be completed first.

The main campaign action receives initial keyboard focus in the opening menu. The shared focus trap still wraps and restores focus. Global campaign shortcuts are guarded while the menu is open. Saved battles and pending after-action reports bypass the opening menu and retain their existing resume/review flow.
