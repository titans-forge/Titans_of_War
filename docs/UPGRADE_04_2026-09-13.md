# Civil War upgrade 04 — Gettysburg landmarks — 0.6.0

Both Gettysburg scenarios now have a distinct historical building. The July 3 battlefield gains Lydia Leister's farmhouse behind Cemetery Ridge; the alternate Jackson battle gains the original Evergreen Cemetery gatehouse beside Cemetery Hill. Eight of the eleven playable battle scenarios now have dedicated physical landmark buildings, alongside Petersburg's existing Crater.

## Art and historical setting

Three native PNG assets were created with the built-in OpenAI image tool: Leister House, Evergreen Gatehouse, and a revised July 3 terrain plate that removes the two generic houses formerly painted into its upper-right corner. The new building sprites retain their original transparent alpha. No exact model identifier was reported. Files were copied unchanged; the game scales and composites them at runtime. The terrain edit retains the original dimensions and major terrain layout, but does not promise identical pixels outside the edited area.

The [NPS Leister farm history](https://www.nps.gov/places/lydia-leister-farm.htm) documents Meade's headquarters, the July 2 council and the July 3 shelling that forced him to relocate. The sprite depicts a small white clapboard dwelling with a shingle roof, chimney and shallow porch. It shows the house standing with an intact porch; shell damage and headquarters evacuation are not simulated. This building is a documented second candidate from the landmark plan. Codori Farm remains a future addition requiring its original buildings to be distinguished from postwar replacements.

The [NPS Evergreen account](https://www.nps.gov/places/gettysburg-then-now-gatehouse.htm) dates the gateway to 1855 and places it at Cemetery Hill. The [NPS then-and-now collection](https://www.nps.gov/gett/learn/photosmultimedia/buildings-and-farms-then-and-now.htm) identifies the later addition on its right side. The new sprite uses the original two brick blocks and central round carriage arch, excluding the later residence and veranda. Roof, trim and unseen surfaces are illustrative. The building is historical; the game's July 1–2 Jackson battle remains explicitly alternate history.

Wartime photographs were inspected within the NPS comparison images, alongside current context photographs. The corresponding Library of Congress records are [Meade's headquarters](https://www.loc.gov/item/2011660054/) and [the cemetery gateway](https://www.loc.gov/item/2006692500/). Direct LOC image downloads returned HTTP 403; downloaded NPS references are retained only in task evidence and are not bundled in the game. Modern roads, memorials and other contemporary details were excluded from the generated buildings.

Exact selected prompts, rejected attempts and original filenames are in `historical-landmark-prompts-v3.json`. Dimensions, hashes, sources and historical qualifications are in `historical-landmark-art-v3.json`. One gatehouse draft had pointed upper windows; its targeted correction painted a checkerboard. A fresh round-window RGBA sprite was selected instead.

## Interaction and compatibility

The July 3 battle keeps its dedicated ridge, wall, reserve and withdrawal rules. Shared landmark access now connects that system to the same movement, pathfinding, group-order previews, firing obstruction, close-combat obstruction, formation-width checks and arrival guards used by the other battles. Adding a building does not convert July 3 into a generic field battle.

The terrain overlay outlines occupied ground, mission labels name each structure, and the inspector provides historical context. Landmark labels sit above these two buildings to keep them separate from nearby sector names. A worn yard and short approach connect Leister House to the rear ground. The gatehouse's small carriage arch is not a traversable route or direct-fire portal for the game's full formations; its note tells players to route around it. There is no garrison, healing, additional cover bonus or destruction mechanic.

Fresh campaigns and quick battles use battle rules 10 in save envelope 14. Existing saves retain their recorded rules and the earlier terrain art. Envelope 13 still accepts only its original versions through rules 9. The existing pre-battle upgrade button explicitly opts an older encounter into rules 10; prior results remain unchanged. Command training retains its earlier rules.
