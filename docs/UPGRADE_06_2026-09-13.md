# Civil War upgrade 06 — Readable siege terrain

Release 0.8.0 · September 13, 2026 · battle rules 12 · campaign save envelope 16.

## What changed

**Cold Harbor:** new generated ravine ground patches make the broken assault area visible. Earthen firing bays and short timber-lined traverses replace the single thin defensive strip. The Petersburg road stays visually continuous. A collapsible terrain/history guide explains crossfire, cover, movement and transfer priorities, with an NPS source link.

**Petersburg:** new elongated Crater art shows a torn clay rim, slumped interior and splintered timber. New earthwork art connects the firing bays to the interior of the existing secondary and Union siege-work zones. The terrain guide identifies Elliott’s Salient and explains reserve timing and sortie objectives. These are representative tactical placements, not surveyed fortifications or a reconstructed covered-way route.

**Crater movement correction:** the art review found an existing inconsistency: the vertical road bonus raised movement inside part of the Crater from 0.72 to 1.16. Rules 12 now keep the entire basin at its existing slow-ground pace of 0.72 and exposure factor 0.82. Roads stop visually at the basin and keep their bonuses outside it. Rules 11 and earlier retain their recorded behavior and receive a clear legacy note in the terrain guide. Save envelope 16 supports the new rules; envelope 15 still rejects rules 12.

**Connected farm lanes:** the old road renderer only handled horizontal or southward vertical segments. It ignored the vertical displacement of diagonal lanes, so short paths could miss their intended destination. It now follows arbitrary endpoints, including northward and westward segments, and clips ruts and shoulders to the declared length and width. This is a visual correction; it creates no new speed bonus.

**Load cost:** each siege battlefield requests only its two relevant new textures. Terrain is composited once when the scene starts; the update adds no per-frame terrain drawing or pixel processing. Other battlefields do not load the new textures.

## Art and sources

Three original generated RGBA PNGs are installed in `public/images/battles/siege-v1/`: `ravine.png`, `earthwork.png`, and `crater.png`. They were made with the built-in image generation tool, which did not report an exact model identifier. Full-resolution originals were copied without pixel edits. Browser compositing was checked for backgrounds and colored edge artifacts. Runtime transforms and clipping fit the authored game terrain zones.

Prompts: `siege-terrain-prompts-v1.json`. Original paths, dimensions, hashes and source qualifications: `siege-terrain-art-v1.json`.

- [NPS Cold Harbor tour](https://www.nps.gov/rich/learn/historyculture/cold-harbor-tour.htm): ravines split the attack columns and exposed them to crossfire.
- [NPS Cold Harbor archeology lesson](https://www.nps.gov/teachers/classrooms/archeology-cold-harbor.htm): soldiers excavated field defenses and used timber stakes.
- [NPS Crater site](https://www.nps.gov/places/the-crater.htm): the elongated mine-blast depression and the trapped attack.
- [NPS battle narrative](https://www.nps.gov/pete/learn/historyculture/battle-of-the-crater.htm) and [historical handbook](https://npshistory.com/handbooks/historical/13/hh13f.htm): Elliott’s Salient, supporting works and the Confederate response.

The geometry and art are interpretive. A depicted traverse adds no wall collision, healing or independent cover rule; protection comes from the existing earthwork zone. Exact boundaries remain available in the tactical overlay.
