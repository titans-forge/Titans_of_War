# Civil War upgrade 14 — Sortie and transfer command

Release 0.16.0 completes the targeted Petersburg C and Cold Harbor D mission checks.

## Player-facing fixes

Petersburg's sortie previously selected an unavailable formation and displayed general Crater-defense instructions. It now selects Mahone's Virginia Brigade and presents a selected-strategy brief, two named target checkpoints, raid deadline 54, return deadline 86, and an orderly-return count. Map mission markers identify the Western Siege Train and Eastern Engineer Depot. View buttons locate either target, pause the battle and preserve selection and orders; viewing remains available after a target has been struck. Failure advice explains the raid and return sequence, and the after-action timeline now includes individual raid events.

Cold Harbor's transfer now selects Breckinridge and identifies the two departing reserves, the screen remaining in the works, the organized-force threshold, the transfer window and the earliest completion time. Its quick-battle, campaign-gate and field briefs agree: send Breckinridge and Hill before minute 54, keep two organized infantry formations holding the works through minute 32, and retain more than 68% of starting strength organized. A wholesale army withdrawal still fails the selected mission.

No combat rules, formations, deployments, score weights, rewards, terrain collision, dependencies or assets changed. This pass adds guidance and camera/marker presentation. Battle rules remain 15 and save envelope remains 19. Earlier rules below 4 retain their prior brief paths.
