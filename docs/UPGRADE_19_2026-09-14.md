# Upgrade 19 — save and resume field command

Release: 0.21.0. Campaign save envelope remains 21; tactical rules remain 17. This implements item 1 of the September 14 remaining-upgrades audit. Items 2–12 remain queued; complete campaign continuity is next.

## Player behavior

Choose **Leave battle → Save and leave** to preserve an active or completed battle on this device. The confirmation names the battle and field time. Reopening the page offers **Resume saved battle**, which restores the encounter paused. A completed result can be saved before returning its consequences to the campaign.

Formations, casualties, ammunition, issued and delayed orders, mission state, reinforcements, enemy coordination, withdrawal state and combat presentation collections are preserved. The renderer rebuilds from the restored engine. Discard remains explicit. Save failures keep the player in the field and show an error.

Field checkpoints use a separate versioned storage entry with a bounded payload and accidental-corruption checksum. The checkpoint is tied to the saved campaign state, preventing a settled result from being replayed against a later campaign. Invalid, incompatible or stale checkpoints show an explanation and preserve the campaign. Campaign settlement clears the checkpoint after successful campaign saving; a failed campaign save retains the prior recovery point.

## Boundaries

This is an explicit save, not continuous battle autosave. Save before closing or refreshing. One field checkpoint is retained per browser origin/device. It is not included in campaign export files. Demo and protected-archive modes retain their existing unsaved field behavior. Camera position, selected inspector, unconfirmed destination previews and sound settings reset; confirmed simulation orders persist. Physical-device testing and cross-device transfers remain future work.
