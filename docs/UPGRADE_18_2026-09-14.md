## Historical basis and limits

The [NPS Tapp Field account](https://www.nps.gov/places/tapp-field.htm) identifies the leased farm, Orange Plank Road and the family's escape. The worked-ground treatment is an illustrative interpretation of a farm, not a documented crop plan or surveyed path.

The [NPS Shiloh Church account](https://www.nps.gov/places/shiloh-church-tour-stop-5.htm) identifies the log meeting house and Sherman's division camping on the ridge for three weeks. The worn assembly area and track evoke that setting; their exact geometry is illustrative.

The [NPS Cold Harbor tour](https://www.nps.gov/rich/learn/historyculture/cold-harbor-tour.htm) places Garthright House behind the Federal line and describes its hospital use after June 3. This pass adds only general farm ground and access, without portraying a later hospital arrangement as present at the battle's opening. The long connection follows the needs of this compressed board, not a verified wartime lane.

Tracks and plots add no movement, cover or hospital bonus. Second named landmarks on these boards remain research work; this pass does not count surroundings as additional historical buildings.
